DellOptiplexTale was started on September 9, 2026 for ACTUAL game development, since July and August were too chaotic. Not much happened between September 10 to 16, until September 17—exactly 4 days before my sister's wedding presentation! The developer opened Sublime Text and created this layout.

When September 9 hit, the developer chose Ren'Py. This game is brought to you by the creator of Saturn Computing Topic, Chris. He is a 12-year-old game programmer born on October 16, 2013, making him one of the youngest independent Filipino game developers out there. He is a dedicated Christian.

Who is Chris?

⚠️ JUST ONE NOTE: I won't show my home address or school. I don't want people finding me!

To describe him basic: he is a massive Church of God Dasma fanboy. If you spot him there, he will be wearing his signature glasses and a protective face mask to keep things completely private.

While creating these games, he was completely BROKE. He started his programming journey way back in Grade 4 at 9 years old using Scratch, running on a beat-up Windows 7 computer built from loose parts in 2022. Since he spent his gift money instantly instead of saving it, the dev budget remains completely empty!

In February 2023, his dad gifted him a computer built around an MSI A68HM-E33 V2 motherboard. Chris absolutely loves old-school legacy tech and never passes up an opportunity to salvage an ancient computer system. His very first machine was a classic HP Compaq d220 MT, which currently sits completely disassembled in parts.

His family circle includes his 9-year-old American cousin, Jaella, who traveled to the Philippines back in 2023. She has been visiting since she was very small, around 2017 to 2019.
